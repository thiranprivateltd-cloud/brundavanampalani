Who I Am

About Me

I am a Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of English specializing in Contemporary Korean Literature, Digital Storytelling, and Webtoons.

My research explores the intersection of traditional narrative ethics and Hallyu Pop aesthetics in Korean Webtoons using Transmedia Storytelling Theory.

Through my academic journey, I aim to understand how literature evolves across digital platforms while preserving cultural identity, ethics, and meaningful storytelling.

I strongly believe that stories have the power to heal, inspire, educate, and connect people beyond geographical and cultural boundaries.

Creative & Academic Profile

Story, Novel, and Poem Writer

In addition to my doctoral research, I am actively engaged as a creative writer, penning stories, novels, and collections of poetry. My works explore the subtle thresholds of human connections, silent emotional healing, and narrative resilience.

Doctoral Work

Research

Advancing Literary Studies through Contemporary Digital Narratives

Ph.D. Research Title

WEBTOONS AS DIGITAL LITERATURE: INTEGRATING HALLYU POP AESTHETICS WITH TRADITIONAL NARRATIVE ETHICS IN CONTEMPORARY KOREAN STORYTELLING

Contemporary Korean Literature Digital Literature Webtoons Narrative Ethics Hallyu Studies Transmedia Storytelling Digital Humanities

Research Objectives

  • 01
    To analyze the representation of betrayal, resilience, empowerment, and personal agency in the selected Korean webtoons.
  • 02
    To examine how the selected webtoons integrate Hallyu pop aesthetics with traditional Confucian narrative ethics in contemporary Korean storytelling.
  • 03
    To establish webtoons as an interdisciplinary form of digital literature by exploring their literary, visual, cultural, and technological dimensions.

Research Interests

Korean Literature Digital Storytelling Webtoons Cultural Studies Media Studies Literary Theory AI in Literature Narrative Ethics

Research Topics

Contemporary Korean Storytelling

Tracing how modern Korean narratives carry inherited literary and philosophical values in an era of globalization.

Narrative Ethics

Investigating the moral weight of interactive story branches and what digital stories owe to their audiences.
